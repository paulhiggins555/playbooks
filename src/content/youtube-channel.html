Overview

This playbook turns your Zoho expertise into a YouTube inbound engine that pulls estate attorneys and existing Zoho users to you automatically. Right now your only lead generation lever is LinkedIn outreach. YouTube becomes your second lever, running in the background 24/7 while you are in delivery.

The strategy is two dedicated channels, not one. Your BoLine/Woggle channel stays attorney-specific. This playbook builds a separate Zoho-focused channel that captures attorneys already on Zoho who have never heard of you. It also targets any Zoho user who might migrate into your niche. Two audiences, two channels, zero algorithm dilution.

YouTube is not a content project. It is a lead capture system you build once and compound over time.

Step 1: Set Up Your Dedicated Zoho Channel

What you are doing: Creating a standalone YouTube account and channel that is completely separate from BoLine. This channel speaks to Zoho users broadly, not estate attorneys specifically.

Why this works: YouTube's algorithm categorises channels by topic. If you mix Zoho generic content with estate attorney-specific content on the same channel, you confuse the algorithm and dilute reach for both audiences. Separate accounts, separate positioning.

How to execute:

  1. Create a new Google account (not your personal Gmail). Name it something channel-appropriate.
  2. Go to YouTube Studio and create a new channel under that account. Name the channel around Zoho and your target vertical, e.g., "Zoho for Professionals" or "Zoho Legal Tech."
  3. Write a channel description that calls out who the channel is for: Zoho users in professional services, legal, and estate planning who want to get more from their CRM and automation.
  4. Link the channel back to your BoLine website in the About section. This is how YouTube traffic finds your product.
  5. Create a simple channel banner. Blue background, your channel name in white, and a one-line value statement. Canva free version is enough.

Common mistakes: Using your personal YouTube account or mixing this with your BoLine channel to "save time." It will cost you reach on both channels.

Pro tip: Add a channel trailer of 60-90 seconds that explains what you cover and who you help. Film it last, once you have your first 3 videos live.

Step 2: Research Competitor Videos Before Filming Anything

What you are doing: Finding what is already working on YouTube in the Zoho space, then building your content list from real search data rather than guessing.

Why this works: The fastest YouTube channels do not invent topics. They find proven topics with existing search demand and produce better or more specific versions. Brett from Zietta and others have already done the hard work of finding what Zoho users search for.

How to execute:

  1. Open a fresh YouTube session (logged out or incognito) so your watch history does not bias results.
  2. Search "Zoho CRM tutorial", "Zoho for attorneys", "Zoho automation", "Zoho vs other CRM." Note which channels appear in the top results.
  3. Open each top creator's channel. Sort their videos by Most Popular. Screenshot or copy the titles of their top 10-15 videos.
  4. Look for patterns: which topics get the most views? Common ones will be setup guides, comparisons, automation how-tos, and migration walkthroughs.
  5. Build a spreadsheet with 20+ potential video titles you could cover better or more specifically, with an estate attorney or legal services angle where relevant.

Common mistakes: Filming topics you think are interesting instead of topics that already have search demand. YouTube rewards relevance, not creativity.

AI Prompt

Use this with your AI system: "Based on the Zoho platform knowledge in your knowledge base, give me 20 YouTube video titles targeting estate planning attorneys who are already using Zoho but are not getting value from it. Format them as search-friendly titles under 60 characters."

Step 3: Build Your First Content Bank (8 Videos)

What you are doing: Scripting and filming 8 videos before publishing any of them. This gives you a buffer and lets you learn the format before going live.

Why this works: Channels that launch with 3-5 videos available immediately get more initial watch time, which signals to YouTube that the channel is worth distributing. Filming in batches is also more efficient than filming one at a time every week.

How to execute:

  1. Pick 8 titles from your research list. Prioritise topics where you can show Zoho doing something that helps estate planning workflows.
  2. Use the script template below for each video. Do not try to improvise for your first 8 videos. Script it, then film.
  3. Record in batches: film all 8 in two or three sessions rather than one per week. You will be surprised how quickly you can crank them out once you are warmed up.
  4. Keep videos between 7 and 12 minutes for tutorial-style content. Under 5 minutes limits watch time. Over 15 minutes scares off beginners.
  5. Screen record Zoho directly whenever you can. Showing the interface does more than talking about it.

Common mistakes: Spending 3 hours editing one video. For a how-to tutorial channel, editing quality matters less than content quality. Cut the dead air, add captions, and publish.

Pro tip: End every video with one CTA only: subscribe, or visit BoLine.com. Not both. Pick one per video and rotate.

Step 4: Publish and Optimise for Search

What you are doing: Publishing your first 3 videos immediately after launch with the right titles, descriptions, and tags to get picked up by YouTube search.

Why this works: YouTube is the second biggest search engine in the world. If you title and describe your videos correctly, people will find them without you promoting them. Most small channels skip this step and wonder why nobody watches.

How to execute:

  1. Title format: lead with the search term, then add context. Example: "Zoho CRM for Estate Attorneys: How to Automate Client Intake in 10 Minutes."
  2. Description: first 2 sentences repeat the title keyword and summarise the video. Then add 3-5 relevant tags written as natural sentences. End with a link to BoLine or your booking page.
  3. Tags: add 10-15 tags mixing broad (Zoho CRM, Zoho tutorial) with specific (Zoho for lawyers, Zoho estate planning, Woggle BoLine).
  4. Thumbnail: use a clean, readable thumbnail. Two-colour background (your blue and orange work well here), large white text, your face if possible. Canva has free YouTube thumbnail templates.
  5. Chapters: add timestamp chapters in the description for videos over 7 minutes. This improves search visibility and watch time.

Common mistakes: Generic titles like "Zoho Tips" or "My Zoho Setup." Nobody searches for those. Lead with the exact problem your viewer has.

Step 5: Use the Migration Angle to Capture Existing Zoho Users

What you are doing: Producing a dedicated playlist for attorneys already on Zoho who want to move to BoLine. This is your highest-value viewer because they are pre-qualified and already bought into the Zoho ecosystem.

Why this works: Zoho will not give you their user list. YouTube search is your way in. Someone searching "migrate from Zoho to BoLine" or "Zoho for estate attorneys review" is at the bottom of the funnel. They just need to find you.

How to execute:

  1. Create a playlist on your Zoho channel titled: "Already on Zoho? Migrate to BoLine."
  2. Film 3 videos specifically for this playlist: (a) What BoLine does that Zoho CRM alone cannot, (b) How migration works step by step, (c) A before/after walkthrough with a real or demo client scenario.
  3. Link this playlist in the end card of every general Zoho video you produce. Viewers who are attorneys will self-select into it.
  4. Use Claude to scrape Reddit and LinkedIn for Zoho complaints from legal professionals. Film a direct response video to the top 3 most common complaints. Title format: "Frustrated with [Zoho pain point]? Here's What We Did Instead."

AI Prompt

Use this with your AI system: "Search for the most common complaints attorneys and legal professionals have about Zoho CRM on Reddit and LinkedIn. Give me the top 5 complaints and for each one, write a YouTube video title and a 3-bullet outline I can film in under 10 minutes."

Step 6: Cross-Link the Two Channels Strategically

What you are doing: Setting up a deliberate pathway from the Zoho channel to the BoLine/Woggle channel so attorney viewers who discover you via generic Zoho content end up on your product-specific channel.

Why this works: Your Zoho channel builds trust and search reach. Your BoLine channel converts. You need both. An attorney who watches 3 of your Zoho videos and then sees a link to "BoLine: Zoho built specifically for estate attorneys" is a pre-warmed prospect.

How to execute:

  1. In every description on the Zoho channel, include: "Estate attorney? We built BoLine specifically for you. See it here: [BoLine YouTube link or website]."
  2. Create one video on the Zoho channel that is explicitly a bridge: "If you're an estate attorney using Zoho, you need to see this." End that video with a direct link to BoLine.
  3. Pinned comment on every Zoho channel video: "Estate attorney using Zoho? BoLine is Zoho pre-built for your practice. [Link]"
  4. Do not cross-link in the other direction. Keep BoLine channel pure for attorneys. The Zoho channel does the net-wide fishing.

Common mistakes: Linking back and forth between both channels on both channels. This confuses the algorithm and the viewer. One-way traffic only: Zoho channel to BoLine.

On Track Check

If you are hitting 40%+ average view duration and 4%+ CTR by Week 4, you are on track. If not, revisit your titles first. Titles drive CTR. If CTR is fine but view duration is low, your hook (first 30 seconds) is losing people. Fix the hook before filming more.

Weekly Execution Routine

Weekly (90 minutes total)

  • Monday (30 min): Pick topic from your content bank, finalise script from template
  • Wednesday (45 min): Film and do basic edit (cut dead air, add captions via YouTube auto-caption or Descript)
  • Thursday (15 min): Upload, fill in title/description/tags from template, schedule publish for Friday 9am

Monthly (30 minutes)

  • Review your top 3 performing videos. What topic, title, or format drove the most views? Film more of that.
  • Check which videos are driving BoLine link clicks. Double down on that content angle.
  • Update your topic backlog with new ideas from Reddit, LinkedIn Zoho conversations, or client questions from that month.
